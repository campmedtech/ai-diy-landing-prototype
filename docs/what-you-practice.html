WHAT YOU PRACTICE

The decision sequence behind useful software delivery.

The six checkpoints you practice in the lab.

1

Clarify the goal

Shape the project vision: what problem are we solving, who is it for, and why does it matter?

(Lab: Vision)

2

Define success

Turn the vision into useful requirements: what must the result do for us to call it successful?

(Lab: Requirements)

3

Break down the work

Choose a focused slice of work that can be built, tested, and reviewed.

(Lab: Build Planning)

4

Build and observe

Let the AI delivery team produce the first working result and inspect what actually came back.

(Lab: Build Execution)

5

Review against expectations

Compare the result to the requirements, acceptance criteria, and user need.

(Lab: Build Review)

6

Decide what comes next

Fix, refine, roll back, or choose the next improvement based on what you learned.
