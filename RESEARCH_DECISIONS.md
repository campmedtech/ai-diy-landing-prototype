# Research decisions

The landing-page hierarchy is based on recurring patterns observed across the current official Product School, Codecademy AI Builder, and Maven AI Product Management course pages.

## Recurring sales-page elements retained on the landing page

- Immediate explanation of the offer and outcome
- A dominant low-friction preview action
- Tangible result or deliverable
- Short explanation of how the experience works
- Audience identification
- Credibility and social proof
- What is included, price, guarantee, and repeated closing action

## Detailed material moved to supporting pages

- Research and extended problem explanation
- Six-stage practice sequence
- Individual simulated-team profiles
- Full founder, partner, company mission, and future-course information on one complete About page

## Visual rule

AI-DIY remains the visual source of truth. The existing logo, typography, navy/white/blue palette, rounded cards, shadows, sticky navigation, hover/touch lift, and button response are preserved. The darker accessible blue and the approved three-button hierarchy remain.

## Copy rule

Important claims, facts, prices, roles, deliverables, guarantees, and differentiators remain. “Your AI Project Team” is applied consistently across the navigation, homepage, and team page. Four short FAQ questions organize existing answers. Repetitive or contextless interface text may be consolidated when the underlying content remains.

## Shared page system

Every supporting page uses the same AI-DIY header, three-action group, Ralph authority strip, closing purchase panel, company statement, footer navigation, and working Contact Us disclosure. Meet Ralph and Partners are anchored sections of the complete About AI-DIY page rather than thin standalone pages.
